# Data card — SITNE-Walk

This card documents every dataset used by SITNE-Walk, its source, license, and
how to obtain it. **The real datasets are not bundled in this repository**;
only the small synthetic `sample_data/` (used for smoke tests) is included.
Large and/or license-restricted data must be fetched or requested as described
below.

## Dataset B — Bernett et al. (redistributable)

- **License**: CC BY 4.0. Redistribution is permitted **with attribution** to
  Bernett et al. (DOI 10.1093/bib/bbae076). Preserve the pinned Q96PU5 overlay
  provenance.
- **Access**: obtain the dataset from the source identified in Bernett et al.
  (DOI 10.1093/bib/bbae076). This repository does not redistribute a copy.
  Place an authorized copy under `02_data_canonical/dataset_B/v1/`.

## Dataset B* — typed PPI (project-derived, CC0)

- **Source versions**: BioGRID 5.0.259 (MIT License) and IntAct R252, as
  recorded in `data_inventory.csv`. The inventory records 2026-07-17 as the
  access date.
- **License**: the project-derived benchmark and reproducibility artifacts are
  released under CC0 1.0. BioGRID 5.0.259 is MIT-licensed. IntAct R252 was
  used as an upstream source; its exact historical license designation was not
  independently reverified, and original IntAct distribution files are not
  redistributed. Original third-party raw database distributions are not
  redistributed.
- **Access**: rebuild locally from the BioGRID and IntAct source records and
  the Bernett split. The training loader enforces a default-deny allow-list
  (`loader_contract.json`); held-out targets must stay locked until the
  training state is frozen.

## External annotations

- `go-basic.obo` — http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/go/go-basic.obo
- `goa_human.gaf.gz` — https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/pub/databases/GO/goa/HUMAN/
- `UniProt2Reactome.txt` — https://reactome.org/download/current/UniProt2Reactome.txt

Place them under `02_data_canonical/external_annotations/` before running
`scripts/run_biological_enrichment.py`.

## Model checkpoints

Training checkpoints are not included in this repository. They can be
regenerated by re-running the training scripts with the required data.
